Welcome to WCE Sangli

Established in 1947, WCE Sangli is a premier autonomous engineering institute affiliated with Shivaji University — blending 77+ years of academic excellence with cutting-edge research, state-of-the-art facilities, and industry partnerships to empower students for a rapidly evolving world.

  • NAAC A+ Accredited · NBA Accredited · NIRF Ranked
  • 300+ companies recruit · ₹1.5 Cr highest package

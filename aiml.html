The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Walchand College of Engineering, Sangli was established in 2024 with a vision to shape the future of technology and innovation. The department focuses on building strong foundational knowledge along with advanced technical expertise in AI and ML. With a curriculum aligned to global standards and industry needs, it emphasizes interdisciplinary learning, problem-solving, and real-world applications. Equipped with modern laboratories, high-performance computing facilities, and advanced software tools, the department provides an excellent academic environment for students to learn, innovate, and excel.

The department prepares students for dynamic careers in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and emerging technologies. Through strong industry collaborations, internships, and research opportunities, students gain hands-on experience and exposure to real-world challenges. Graduates are highly sought-after by leading organizations such as Google, Amazon, and Microsoft. With a focus on ethical AI, innovation, and lifelong learning, the department aims to develop globally competent professionals capable of addressing complex societal and industrial challenges.
